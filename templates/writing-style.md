# Writing style — drafting guide for <owner>'s voice

## How this file works
- **Capture, then promote.** After the owner finalizes a draft, append a dated capture below: the model's draft, the owner's final, and the lessons from the diff. A lesson becomes a numbered pattern only after it appears in two or more independent captures (different letters, ideally different recipients or genres). One capture is an observation, not a pattern.
- **Patterns are moves, not vocabulary.** State each pattern as a principle, with wording as illustration only. Word-level lessons are never promoted as words to use, only as vetoes.
- **Voice only.** Record how the owner says things, never habits the owner wants to change. Those go under "Observed, not emulated."
- **Each capture** carries a genre tag (scheduling, negotiation, outreach, deliverable, personal) and ends with a one-line retention note (rough share of the model's draft kept verbatim). Retention should trend up if the guide is working.
- **Scale triggers:** at about thirty captures, rotate older ones to a dated archive file; at about twenty patterns or two that conflict, run a consolidation pass; a pattern the owner contradicts twice is revised or retired with a dated note.
